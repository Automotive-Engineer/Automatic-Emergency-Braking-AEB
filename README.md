# Automatic Emergency Braking (AEB) System

## Overview
This project focuses on the modeling, simulation, and development of an Automatic Emergency Braking (AEB) system for vehicle collision avoidance.

## Current Work
- Implemented and evaluated multiple control strategies for AEB.
- Compared controller performance under different simulation scenarios.
- Analyzed the system response and braking behavior.

## Controller Design and Comparison

Implemented and compared four classical control strategies for the Automatic Emergency Braking (AEB) system:

- **P Controller** — Proportional control.
- **PI Controller** — Proportional-Integral control.
- **PD Controller** — Proportional-Derivative control.
- **PID Controller** — Proportional-Integral-Derivative control.

The controllers were evaluated through simulation to compare their system responses and braking performance.

## Future Development

Further development will focus on enhancing the TTC-based braking strategy by considering additional parameters, including vehicle mass, road gradient, stopping distance, and lead vehicle speed.
## Ongoing Development
The next development stage focuses on enhancing the Time-to-Collision (TTC)-based braking strategy by investigating the influence of additional parameters, including:

- Vehicle mass.
- Road slope or gradient.
- Stopping distance.
- Lead vehicle speed.

## Objectives
- Compare the performance of different AEB controllers.
- Investigate the effect of vehicle and road parameters on braking decisions.
- Improve the robustness of TTC-based collision avoidance logic.
- Evaluate system behavior through simulation.
